Sankofa EcoVillage Initiative

Sankofa represents our commitment to sustainable development and cultural preservation. This pioneering initiative combines eco-friendly practices with African heritage, creating a model for community-based sustainable living.

By returning to our roots (Sankofa means "go back for it" in Twi), we're building a blueprint for future development that honors tradition while embracing innovation.
